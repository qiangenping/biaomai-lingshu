# BiaoMai Lingshu Marketplace — 70/30 Developer Revenue-Share Program

To build the largest open-source AI workbench ecosystem alongside global developers, **BiaoMai Lingshu** operates an open, creator-friendly **70/30 Revenue-Share Marketplace** for commercial Industry Solution Packs (`.bmpack`).

## What You Can Monetize
- **Vertical Lead-Gen Packs**: Cross-border e-commerce TikTok kits, real-estate / SaaS / agency B2B acquisition playbooks.
- **Local Office & Productivity Packs**: Financial spreadsheet auditors, legal/bidding document reviewers, multilingual PPT builders.
- **Enterprise ToB Connectors**: ERP, CRM, OA, and compliance MCP bridges.

## Economics & Payouts
- **70% to the Pack Author**, **30% Platform Fee** (covers global Stripe/Paddle + WeChat/Alipay checkout, tax/MoR compliance, CDN distribution, and license key issuance).
- Free/open-source packs (Apache-2.0 / MIT) are hosted at **0% fee** with featured placement in the Official Marketplace.

## Submission Requirements
1. Valid `aion-extension.json` passing `pnpm verify:packs` and `pnpm verify:i18n`.
2. Zero copyleft (GPL/AGPL) or unauthorized scraping code (`pnpm verify:licenses` clean).
3. Explicit minimal `permissions` manifest and Human-in-the-Loop approval on external writes.
