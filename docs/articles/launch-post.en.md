# Introducing BiaoMai Lingshu: The Open-Source, Extensible AI Workbench Built for Global Lead Generation & ToB Workflows

Closed-source AI desktop assistants lock enterprises into black-box models, rigid UIs, and fragile browser extensions. Today we are open-sourcing **BiaoMai Lingshu (`biaomai-operations-platform`)** under **Apache-2.0**.

### Standing on the Shoulders of Giants (Zero Wheel Reinvention)
Instead of rewriting desktop shells, agent loops, or OAuth connectors from scratch, BiaoMai Lingshu unifies four battle-tested permissive open-source foundations:
- **`iOfficeAI/AionUi` (Apache-2.0)** for cross-platform desktop UI, 24/7 Cron automation, and Feishu/WeChat/Telegram remote control.
- **`anomalyco/opencode` (MIT) & `openai/codex` (Apache-2.0)** for autonomous agent execution.
- **`yikart/AiToEarn` (MIT)** for 14+ global social channel publishing, multimodal generation, and short-link attribution.
- **Embedded Isolated Chromium + `vercel-labs/agent-browser` + `browserbase/stagehand`** for an AI-native browser with zero external Chrome extension setup.

### Start with Lead Generation Today, Expand to Any ToB Workflow Tomorrow
V1.0 ships with `pack-leadgen-ops` (`Create → Publish → Engage → Convert CRM & ROI`). Thanks to our declarative `.bmpack` SDK and ToB permission sandbox, teams can hot-plug Local Office automation, Bidding Review, or internal ERP/CRM packs without touching the micro-kernel.
